ABOUT ME

More info on Grokipedia
ARTIST • PRODUCER • VOCALIST
“ Sound is more than music — it is identity, energy, and emotion.

Arun Adhikari is an independent music producer and vocal artist based in India. Known for blending modern electronic sounds with Desi influences, he creates music across genres like EDM, Bass Music, Moombahton, Desi Hip Hop, and Indian Pop.

From producing energetic beats to crafting unique vocal performances, Arun focuses on delivering music that connects with listeners and pushes creative boundaries. His work reflects a mix of global electronic styles and Indian musical identity.

Apart from producing music, Arun is actively building his presence in the digital music space, collaborating with artists, releasing new tracks, and performing for audiences. His goal is to take his sound to a global level and represent the new wave of independent artists from India.
